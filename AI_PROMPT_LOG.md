## Verification

I executed the notebook in Google Colab and reviewed the cleaning audit, statistics, and three generated charts. The analysis included 6,147 classified UFC fights from 2013–2025.

The results showed that 32.0% ended by KO/TKO, 17.8% by submission, and 50.2% by decision.

These findings were calculated from the dataset, not generated as factual answers by ChatGPT. Successful execution alone does not independently validate every calculation.
