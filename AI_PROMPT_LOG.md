## Verification

I ran the notebook in Google Colab and checked the cleaning table, statistics, and three charts. After cleaning, I ended up with 6,147 UFC fights from 2013–2025.

According to my results, 32.0% of fights ended by KO/TKO, 17.8% by submission, and 50.2% by decision.

I used the notebook's calculations for these numbers rather than relying on ChatGPT to provide the results.
